**Implementation Tips**
1. **Low‑Barrier Entry** – Use no‑code tools (Zapier, Integromat) for integration whenever possible.
2. **Local Languages** – Offer Arabic & French UI to cater to the majority of staff.
3. **Pricing** – Start with a one‑time setup fee; offer monthly maintenance at 5–10 MAD per table or per month.
4. **Pilot Projects** – Offer a 30‑day free trial for the first 3 restaurants to build testimonials.
5. **Compliance** – Ensure data handling follows GDPR and Moroccan data protection laws.
